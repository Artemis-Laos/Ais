Why families choose AIS

International standards, proven in Laos.

Every claim below can be checked. It is how we show parents that an AIS education meets the same bar as schools in Sydney or Cambridge.

Only in Laos

AIS is the only school in the country offering NAPLAN, Australia’s national literacy and numeracy assessment.

Cambridge registered

A registered Cambridge International School and an authorised Cambridge English preparation and test centre.

SIMCC partner

Since 2017, the local partner of the Singapore International Math Contests Centre, taking Lao students to international olympiads.

Since 2011

Founded within Sengsavanh Education Group, which has been teaching in Vientiane since 1988.

The AIS pathway

One school, from Nursery to university.

Turn the wheel to follow your child’s journey, one stage at a time.

Nursery to K3 · Preschool

Hands-on, play-based learning

Children learn by doing: science experiments, gardening, cooking, performing arts. Lessons are built to make them curious about the world and eager to come to school.

  • Play-based program designed around creativity and exploration
  • Carefully selected teachers with regular development seminars
  • CCTV access for parents and nutritious meals with no MSG
Explore Preschool →

Year 1 to 7 · Primary

The New South Wales Australian curriculum

A balance of direct teaching and inquiry-based learning, backed by a strong co-curricular program. English, mathematics, science, ICT, the arts and more.

  • The only school in Laos offering Australia’s NAPLAN assessment, in Year 3 and Year 5
  • Results show parents exactly where their child stands against Australian standards
  • Foundation program for students who are new to English
Explore Primary →

Year 8 to 10 · Cambridge IGCSE

The world’s most popular international qualification

Students study English, mathematics, co-ordinated science, business studies and design & technology, with Chinese, Korean or computer science as electives.

  • Taken by almost 500,000 students in 144 countries every year
  • AIS is a registered Cambridge International School
  • After-school tutorials and exam preparation coaching
Explore Secondary →

Year 11 to 12 · AS & A Level

Two strands, one goal: a place at university

Students choose a Business strand or a Science strand and graduate with qualifications accepted by leading universities worldwide.

  • Business strand: business studies, economics, design & technology
  • Science strand: biology, physics, chemistry
  • Evening IELTS preparation available

After Year 12 · University

From Vientiane to campuses around the world

Through our partner 1st Class Education, families get free guidance on choosing a degree, applications, visas and accommodation.

  • A network of 46 universities in Australia, Canada, New Zealand, the UK and Europe
  • Help with applications, visas, insurance and airport pick-up
  • Full scholarships won at Dhurakij Pundit University, Bangkok

Joining AIS takes three steps.

Assessments run from May to July each year. Contact us at any time and we will guide you through it.

  1. 1

    Placement test

    Your child sits short tests in reading, writing and mathematics so we can place them at the right level.

  2. 2

    Family interview

    Parents and student meet our team. You ask your questions, we get to know your child.

  3. 3

    School reference

    We review the record and reference from your child’s previous school.
