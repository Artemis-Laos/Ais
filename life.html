International olympiads and competitions

From a classroom in Vientiane to a podium abroad.

Since 2017, AIS has been the local partner for the Singapore International Math Contests Centre (SIMCC), through the Singapore Scholastic Trust Teachers’ Institute. SIMCC is one of the largest math and science contest organisers in Singapore and Asia.

Our olympiads are first organised at the local level and are open to students from other schools. Through them we bring children from Laos to international academic contests around the world.
