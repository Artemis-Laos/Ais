Preschool

Nursery to Kindergarten 3. Create, discover, explore: a hands-on, play-based start to school.

Our program

Children should have fun while they learn.

That idea is the whole basis of our preschool curriculum, and it is why we built a hands-on, play-based program. Young children are given experiences that develop creativity and exploration, so that curiosity about the world and eagerness to study take root early.

We do this not simply by giving children information, but by encouraging them and giving them the freedom to explore. Activities are structured to bring the world into the classroom and to build cognitive, social, emotional and physical skills.

What children do

Ten subjects, all taught through play.

  • English
  • Math
  • Science experiments
  • Discovery
  • Performing arts
  • Arts and craft
  • Physical education
  • Gardening
  • Cooking
  • First aid

What parents ask first

Teachers, safety and health.

Our teachers

Preschool teachers are carefully selected, and their background, education and work references are verified. They are monitored and attend regular teacher development seminars.

Your child’s safety

Parents have CCTV access to their child’s classroom. Teachers supervise the campus during every break, at lunch and after school, and parents are encouraged to visit classes and teachers.

Your child’s health

Snacks and lunches are nutritious and tasty, and never contain MSG. Games, exercise and dance are part of every week.
