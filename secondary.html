Curriculum

Prepared for life, not only for exams.

AIS provides a well-rounded program of instruction for a diverse, international student body. The Cambridge program helps students develop an informed curiosity and a lasting passion for learning.

Year 8 to 10

Cambridge IGCSE

Cambridge IGCSE is the world’s most popular international qualification. It attracts almost 500,000 students from 144 countries every year.

Core subjects

  • English
  • Mathematics
  • Co-ordinated science
  • Business studies
  • Design & technology

Electives

  • Chinese
  • Korean
  • Computer science

Year 11 to 12

Cambridge AS & A Level

AS and A Levels give students access to leading universities in the UK, Ireland, the USA, Canada, New Zealand and Australia. Students choose one of two strands.

Business strand

  • English
  • Math
  • Business studies
  • Economics
  • Design & technology

Science strand

  • English
  • Math
  • Biology
  • Physics
  • Chemistry

Both strands can add an elective: Chinese, Korean or computer science.
