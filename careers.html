Working at AIS

Teach in a Cambridge-accredited school in Vientiane, with students who are eager to learn.

Roles we recruit for

Teaching positions

These are the roles AIS most recently advertised. To ask about current openings, send your CV and a cover letter to admin.manager@aisedulaos.com.

Secondary Mathematics Teacher

IGCSE and A Level · Secondary · 2-year renewable contract

What you will do

  • Plan and deliver engaging mathematics lessons to students from diverse cultural backgrounds
  • Differentiate instruction to meet varying needs and English proficiency levels
  • Prepare students to excel in their IGCSE and AS/A Level exams
  • Assess, record and report on student progress, with prompt feedback
  • Take part in clubs, field trips, performances and academic competitions

What we are looking for

  • Native-level or highly proficient English, written and spoken
  • A bachelor’s degree in education with a mathematics specialisation, or a mathematics degree
  • Knowledge of the Australian or Cambridge curriculum; experience preferred
  • Newly qualified teachers are welcome

Secondary English Teacher

IGCSE, A Level and IELTS · Secondary · 2-year renewable contract

What you will do

  • Plan and deliver engaging English lessons to ESL learners from diverse cultural backgrounds
  • Create a supportive, inclusive environment that builds language and cultural awareness
  • Work with colleagues on cross-curricular projects
  • Contribute to assessments, reports and parents’ evenings

What we are looking for

  • Native-speaker level English with a teaching qualification (TEFL, TESOL, CELTA or PGCE)
  • A bachelor’s degree in education or an English-related field
  • Experience teaching ESL learners, ideally in an international school
  • Familiarity with the Cambridge curriculum and IELTS

Primary English Teacher

Cambridge Checkpoint and ESL · Primary · 2-year renewable contract

What you will do

  • Plan and deliver engaging English lessons to young ESL learners
  • Create attractive teaching material matched to the curriculum and class level
  • Maintain a classroom environment that supports good learning
  • Contribute to assessments, reports and parents’ evenings

What we are looking for

  • Native-speaker level English with a teaching qualification (TEFL, TESOL, CELTA or PGCE)
  • A bachelor’s degree in education or an English-related field
  • Experience teaching ESL learners, ideally in an international school
  • Familiarity with the Cambridge curriculum and assessment standards

What teachers receive

  • Tax-free net salary, paid monthly in USD
  • Work visa and resident permit provided
  • Professional development opportunities
  • School lunch provided
  • Health insurance included
  • Paid public holidays

Safeguarding

AIS is committed to safeguarding the welfare of children and young people, and expects the same from its staff. All new staff go through pre-employment clearance: identity, criminal background, qualification and employment checks, plus two verified professional references.

How to apply

Email your CV, cover letter and referees’ contact details to admin.manager@aisedulaos.com. Applications are reviewed as they arrive, and shortlisted candidates are contacted for interview.
