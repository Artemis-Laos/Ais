1st Class Education

Thinking about study abroad, but not sure where to start?

First Class Education helps at every step, from finding the right degree to the visa application to finding somewhere to live. As a member of Study Group, it represents a network of 46 universities in Australia, Canada, New Zealand, the UK and Europe.

Free services for AIS families

  • A personal assessment and information on your study options
  • Help with applications to partner universities and language centres
  • Help arranging accommodation at your study destination
  • Help with the visa application when studying at a partner institution
  • Help with overseas insurance and airport pick-up

An IELTS preparation course is also available.
