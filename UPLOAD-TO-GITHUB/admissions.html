How to apply

Our procedure has three parts.

Assessments take place from May until July. Candidates take a series of tests in reading, writing and mathematics.

  1. Placement test

    Reading, writing and mathematics, so your child starts at the right level.

  2. Parent and student interview

    A conversation with our team, and time for all of your questions.

  3. Previous school reference

    We review your child’s record from their current or previous school.

Documents you need

Bring these five things.

Have them ready when you come for the interview and enrolment will be quick.

Start your application →
  • Three 3×3 photos each of the student and the parent or guardian
  • A copy of the passport
  • Certificate of residency
  • Previous school record
  • Certificate of enrolment from the previous school

Discounts

Ways to pay less.

Discounts apply to tuition fees only.

  1. 10% for paying in full

    When the year’s tuition is paid in full before 31 July.

  2. 15 to 20% for siblings

    15% for a second sibling and 20% for the third to fifth, when paid in full.

  3. 30% for government employees

    When the year’s tuition is paid in full.
