Director’s welcome

Dear parents,

The choice of a school is both important and difficult for parents, since it determines your children’s future. We hope you will see AIS as a great place of learning and growth for your child. We are constantly working hard to ensure that the parents who choose our school are happy and satisfied with their choice.

In addition to academic growth, our school makes sure that students grow physically, socially, spiritually and emotionally happy, in a safe and secure environment.

At AIS, we believe that every student possesses the potential to excel.

That is why our school provides high-quality academic, co-curricular and support programs. Our aim is for every student to make continuous progress and reach their full potential.

As part of our commitment to excellence, AIS is a member of the Cambridge International School network. We have been granted both Cambridge International Education assessment accreditation and authorisation as a Cambridge Assessment English preparation and test centre.

We look forward to welcoming you to our school.

Sakdina RosantDirector and Co-founder

Our history

Part of a group that has been teaching since 1988.

Sengsavanh Education Group is a privately owned group founded by Dr. Khamsen Sisavong. It was one of the first educational institutions in Laos to offer a bilingual program, and today runs programs from preschool to higher education.

AIS grew out of the vision of Dr. Khamsen Sisavong and co-founder Mrs. Sakdina Rosant: a centre for excellence that meets international standards and serves the Lao people and expatriates alike, preparing students for the changing demands of life in the 21st century.

The group’s campuses in Vientiane

  • SV Institute of Business (SIB)
  • Sengsavanh Bilingual School 1 (SBS 1)
  • Sengsavanh Bilingual School 2 (SBS 2)
  • The Australian International School, City campus
  • The Australian International School, Boarding campus
  1. 1988

    Sengsavanh Language Center opens in Vientiane.

  2. 1999

    The centre becomes Sengsavanh College. A preschool, primary and high school open the same year, with a bilingual program.

  3. 2011

    The Australian International School is founded.

  4. 2017

    AIS becomes the local partner of the Singapore International Math Contests Centre.

  5. Today

    Sengsavanh College offers diploma, BA, BBA and MBA programs, and AIS teaches Nursery to Year 12 on its City and Boarding campuses.

Our mission

Excellence in education, in a place where every student feels safe.

We aim to create an inclusive environment where all students feel cared for, comfortable making mistakes, and challenged. Our mission is to ensure that students become proficient in English, possess the academic skills required for further education overseas, and are future-ready. We aim to go beyond what is expected of us to support every single one of our students.

Our vision

To become the leading educational institution in Laos.

We want our students to achieve academic excellence, perform to the best of their ability, and become healthy, productive, successful individuals. We aspire to develop lifelong learners and global citizens who will contribute to the development of their country and believe they can change the world.
