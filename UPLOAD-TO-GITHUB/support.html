Academic support and welfare

Every student should have what they need to reach their full potential. Here is how we make sure of it.

  • New to English
  • Learning support

Non-English speaking students

A foundation program built around English.

The foundation program puts a strong emphasis on building English proficiency and is set by stage of learning. Students are taught English as a second language, mathematics and science, with English as the language of instruction.

Foundation teachers adjust the regular program to each student’s needs, and ESL learners can also draw on specialised teachers, school support programs and individual tutorials.

Learning support

An individual plan when a student needs one.

For students experiencing academic difficulties, or who simply need additional help, our specialists develop an Individual Education Plan (IEP) and adjust the regular program to fit.

Our support programs

  • After-school homework club
  • Supervised help sessions
  • After-school ESL classes
  • After-school subject tutorials
  • After-school exam preparation coaching
  • Study skills coaching
  • Individual intervention program

Student welfare

Children learn when they feel safe and cared for.

Teaching is the prime concern of any school. But successful learning only happens in an environment where children feel safe, secure and cared for.

Student welfare is the responsibility of every teacher and member of staff, and a dedicated team provides additional support. Every member of the AIS community knows their role in safeguarding and promoting student welfare.

Department principals

Lead welfare in each part of the school.

Special educational needs specialists

Plan and adjust learning for individual students.

School psychologists

Support students’ emotional wellbeing.

Counsellor

Someone to talk to, whenever a student needs it.
