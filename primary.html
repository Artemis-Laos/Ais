Primary

Year 1 to Year 7, following the New South Wales Australian curriculum.

Curriculum

Direct teaching and inquiry, in balance.

The Australian International School offers the New South Wales (NSW) Australian curriculum from Year 1 to Year 7. It combines direct teaching with inquiry-based learning and is supported by a strong co-curricular program.

  • Mathematics

  • Design and technology
  • The arts

Assessment

The only school in Laos offering NAPLAN

The National Assessment Program for Literacy and Numeracy is run by the Australian Curriculum, Assessment and Reporting Authority (ACARA). AIS students sit it in Year 3 and Year 5.

NAPLAN assesses numeracy, reading, writing, spelling, punctuation and grammar. The results tell parents how their child is achieving, and they are a guarantee that AIS follows Australian standards.

New to English?

A foundation program gets students ready.

Students who do not yet speak English join our foundation program, which teaches English as a second language alongside mathematics and science, all in English and set by stage of learning.
