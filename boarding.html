Boarding

Separate boys’ and girls’ houses on both campuses, with tutors, activities and round-the-clock care.

Why board?

Independence, learned one day at a time.

Parents are increasingly busy with work and often have to travel, which makes it hard to keep a close eye on a child’s study and routine.

In our boarding houses, students do their own laundry, get themselves up in the morning, and attend homework support classes, tutorials and sports training. That builds initiative and responsibility. Living with others builds strong social skills too, and the experience tends to produce more mature, independent, disciplined and open-minded young people.

Academic support for boarders

All boarders work with a team of tutors who specialise in lifting their academic performance.

Facilities

Where boarders live

  • Shared or single rooms
  • Common learning space
  • Recreation space
  • Cooking area
  • Supervised access to all school sports facilities

Services

What is included

  • Homework support and tutorials
  • Sports clubs and weekend activities
  • Local, Western, Chinese, vegan and halal meals
  • 24-hour security
  • Cleaning service
  • Full-time nurse on site

Support for Chinese students

Extra care for students far from home.

We understand that the cultural difference can be hard for Chinese students, and our boarders’ health, happiness and wellbeing matter most to us. Chinese students have a designated communication officer, extra English and Lao classes, access to Chinese TV channels, and Chinese meals prepared specially for them.

Boarding (school dormitory) is charged monthly. See the fee schedule.
